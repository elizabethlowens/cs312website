I'm especially motivated by roles where I can make a tangible impact and contribute to something larger than myself.

The connection

The discipline I build in my academic and career pursuits strengthens my confidence and independence, while my personal values keep me grounded and focused on what matters. I've learned that success isn't just about achievements; it's also about staying true to who I am and the kind of person I want to become. That balance is something I'm still learning, but it's also something I'm proud of.
